Before you start

  • Sit well supported at a table, with your feet flat on the floor.
  • If the pen is hard to hold, a thicker pen or a foam grip can make it easier.
  • Tilt the paper slightly and steady it with your free hand or a clip.

These sheets come from ReWrite, a handwriting practice app for adults relearning to write after a stroke, with Parkinson's, MS, dystonia or another condition that has changed their writing.

You can try it in your browser — it also makes sheets from your own words, names and addresses.

ReWrite is a wellbeing and self-management tool, not a medical device.

Does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Always follow your clinician's guidance.
