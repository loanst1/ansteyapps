Three things worth doing before you start

  • Sit properly at a table with your feet flat on the floor. Writing on your lap makes everything harder.
  • If the pen is hard to hold, a thicker barrel or a foam grip usually helps more than trying harder.
  • Tilt the paper slightly and hold it with your other hand or a clip.

These sheets come from ReWrite, a handwriting practice app for adults relearning to write after stroke, with Parkinson's, MS, dystonia or another condition that has changed their writing. You can try it in your browser — it also makes sheets from your own words, names and addresses. More about handwriting and Parkinson's, or read the free guides.
