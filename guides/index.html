Guides

Practical, evidence-informed guides to recovering vision and handwriting skills after stroke — what changes, why, and the exercises you can practise at home.

Visual field exercises after stroke (hemianopia)

The compensatory scanning exercises that help you read and move around after losing part of your visual field.

Handwriting exercises after stroke

A stroke can leave handwriting shaky, slow or hard to read. Learn the graded exercises — from warm-ups to letters, words and sentences — that help rebuild control at home.

Visual neglect after stroke

Visual (hemispatial) neglect means not attending to one side of space, even though the eyes work. Learn how it differs from hemianopia and the scanning exercises that help.

Reading problems after stroke

Losing part of your visual field can make reading slow and frustrating. Learn the anchoring and scanning strategies that help you find the start and end of each line.

These guides are general information, not medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your own rehabilitation team.
