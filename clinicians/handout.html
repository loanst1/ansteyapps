Practising at home — Stroke Sight & ReWrite

Your rehabilitation team has suggested one of these apps to help you keep practising at home, in your own language.

For changes to your vision

Stroke Sight

For visual field loss after stroke (hemianopia, quadrantanopia, visual neglect). Short exercises that train your eyes to scan into the affected side, with ten levels and progress you can bring to your appointments.

Scan with your phone camera, or type:ansteyapps.com/e/ss
For handwriting

ReWrite

For handwriting difficulty after stroke or neurological injury. Guided tracing of letters, numbers, words and sentences — in your own script — to rebuild control and confidence.

Scan with your phone camera, or type:ansteyapps.com/e/rw
